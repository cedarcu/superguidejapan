Top 10 Must-Try Foods in Japan

1. Ramen (Tonkotsu, Shoyu, Miso)

Try Ichiran for the solo-dining experience, or seek out local, independent shops for regional specialties like Hakata Tonkotsu or Sapporo Miso.

2. Sushi (Kaitenzushi & Omakase)

Experience the fun of conveyor belt sushi at Kura Sushi or Sushiro, and treat yourself to a high-end Omakase experience at least once.

3. Wagyu Beef (Kobe, Matsusaka, Hida)

Melt-in-your-mouth premium Japanese beef. Enjoy it as steak, in Sukiyaki, or grilled at a Yakiniku restaurant.

4. Okonomiyaki

A savory pancake made with cabbage, meat, and seafood. Try the layered Hiroshima style or the mixed Kansai style.

5. Takoyaki

Osaka's famous street food: molten balls of dough filled with octopus, topped with sauce, mayo, and bonito flakes. Be careful, they are served incredibly hot!

6. Tempura

Lightly battered and deep-fried seafood and vegetables. Best eaten fresh at a specialized Tempura counter.

7. Tonkatsu

Thick, breaded, and deep-fried pork cutlet. Often served with shredded cabbage and a rich, tangy sauce.

8. Matcha Everything

From traditional tea ceremonies in Kyoto to matcha parfaits, ice cream, and modern lattes.

9. Yakitori

Grilled chicken skewers. Perfect with a cold beer at a lively Izakaya or under the train tracks in Yurakucho.

10. Convenience Store (Konbini) Snacks

Don't miss the Egg Sandwiches at 7-Eleven, Famichiki (fried chicken) at FamilyMart, and Karaage-kun at Lawson.
