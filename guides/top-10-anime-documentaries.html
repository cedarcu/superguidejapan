Top 10 Documentaries on Anime & Manga

1. The Kingdom of Dreams and Madness

A beautiful, melancholic behind-the-scenes look at Studio Ghibli during the production of "The Wind Rises".

2. Never-Ending Man: Hayao Miyazaki

Follows the legendary director as he comes out of retirement (again) to experiment with CGI animation.

3. Hideaki Anno: The Final Challenge of Evangelion

An intense, unprecedented look at the troubled but brilliant mind of the Evangelion creator during its final film.

4. Urasawa Naoki no Manben

A fascinating NHK documentary series where famous mangaka (comic artists) are filmed drawing, revealing their unique techniques.

5. Enter the Anime (Netflix)

A fast-paced introduction to the world of anime production, featuring interviews with creators from Castlevania, Aggretsuko, and Kengan Ashura.

6. Otaku Unite!

An older but important documentary tracing the history and evolution of anime fandom in the West.

7. 10 Years with Hayao Miyazaki (NHK)

An extensive 4-part series documenting a decade of Miyazaki's life, creative process, and relationship with his son Goro.

8. Satoshi Kon: The Illusionist

A touching tribute to the late, great director of Perfect Blue and Paprika, featuring interviews with Mamoru Oshii and Darren Aronofsky.

9. Shinkai Makoto Documentary

Various television specials cover the meticulous digital creation process behind hits like Your Name and Weathering with You.

10. The Great Passage (Fune wo Amu) Making-of

While technically a featurette for the anime series, it provides a fascinating look into the daily grind of modern TV anime production.
