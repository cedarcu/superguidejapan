Top 10 Legendary Anime Directors & Mangaka

1. Osamu Tezuka

The "God of Manga". Creator of Astro Boy, Black Jack, and Kimba the White Lion. He practically invented the modern manga industry.

2. Hayao Miyazaki

Co-founder of Studio Ghibli. Known for Spirited Away, Princess Mononoke, and his staunch dedication to traditional hand-drawn animation.

3. Akira Toriyama

The mastermind behind Dragon Ball and Dr. Slump. His dynamic art style also defined the look of the Dragon Quest video game series.

4. Makoto Shinkai

Often hailed as the "Next Miyazaki," Shinkai is famous for his hyper-realistic, breathtakingly beautiful background art (Your Name, Suzume).

5. Hideaki Anno

Creator of Neon Genesis Evangelion. He redefined the mecha genre and brought deep psychological themes into mainstream anime.

6. Satoshi Kon

A visionary director known for seamless transitions between reality and illusion (Paprika, Perfect Blue, Paranoia Agent).

7. Rumiko Takahashi

One of the wealthiest and most successful female mangaka, creator of Urusei Yatsura, Ranma ½, and Inuyasha.

8. Naoki Urasawa

The master of suspense manga, responsible for incredibly complex and gripping thrillers like Monster, 20th Century Boys, and Pluto.

9. Katsuhiro Otomo

Creator and director of Akira. His detailed architectural art and fluid animation standards changed the industry forever.

10. Eiichiro Oda

Creator of One Piece, the best-selling manga series in history. Known for his unparalleled world-building and dedication to his craft.
