How to Apply as a Foreigner Without Rejection

Applying for credit cards in Japan can be frustrating for foreigners. Follow these strict rules to ensure approval:

  • Name Consistency: Your name on the application MUST exactly match your bank account and Residence Card (Zairyu Card). If your bank uses Katakana, use the exact same Katakana.
  • Bank Linkage: Link your bank account online during the application rather than by mail. Yucho Bank (Japan Post) or Rakuten Bank are highly recommended.
  • Income Declaration: Be honest but ensure you meet the minimum thresholds. Students should declare part-time income or family support.
  • Phone Number: You must have a valid Japanese phone number starting with 070, 080, or 090 (050 IP numbers are often rejected).

Point Maximization (Poi-katsu / ポイ活)

Once you have the card, it’s time to maximize points (1 point = 1 Yen):

  • SPU (Super Point Up) Program: Combine Rakuten services (Card, Mobile, Bank, Securities) to increase your base multiplier up to 16x on Rakuten Ichiba.
  • 0 and 5 Day Sales: ONLY buy on Rakuten Ichiba on days ending in 0 or 5 (5th, 10th, 15th, 20th, 25th, 30th) using your Rakuten Card for massive bonus multipliers.
  • Shopping Marathons (お買い物マラソン): Wait for these monthly events. Buying from 10 different shops multiplies your total points by 10x.
