Student Visa vs SSW Visa (特定技能) & Initial Costs

Student Visa (留学)

  • Purpose: Studying at a Japanese language school, vocational school (senmon gakkou), or university.
  • Work Restriction: The strict 28-hour work rule. You must apply for "Shikakugai Katsudo Kyoka" (Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted under the Status of Residence Previously Granted) to work up to 28 hours a week (up to 40 hours during long school holidays).
  • Initial Costs: High. Expect to pay 6 months to 1 year of tuition upfront (approx. 400,000 to 800,000 JPY), plus flights, housing initial costs, and bringing at least 150,000 JPY in pocket money.

Specified Skilled Worker (SSW / 特定技能) Visa

  • Purpose: Full-time work in designated industries facing labor shortages (caregiving, food service, construction, agriculture, etc.).
  • Requirements: Passing a Japanese language test (JLPT N4 or JFT-Basic) and an industry-specific skills test.
  • Work Restriction: You are a full-time employee. No 28-hour limit, but you are tied to the specific industry category you passed the test for.
  • Initial Costs: Lower than student visas as there are no school tuition fees, though you still need money for flights, initial housing, and living expenses until your first paycheck.

Pro Tip for Students

Do not violate the 28-hour rule. Immigration checks tax and bank records during visa renewals. If caught exceeding the limit, you face deportation and will not be able to change to a work visa after graduation.
