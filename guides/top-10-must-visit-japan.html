Top 10 Must-Visit Iconic Spots in Japan

1. teamLab Planets (Tokyo)

Immerse yourself in digital art where you walk barefoot through water. A truly surreal experience in Toyosu.

Book Tickets on Klook →

2. Fushimi Inari Taisha (Kyoto)

Walk through thousands of vermilion torii gates winding up the sacred Mount Inari. Go early in the morning to beat the crowds.

3. Universal Studios Japan (Osaka)

Home to the incredible Super Nintendo World and the Wizarding World of Harry Potter.

Get Express Passes on Klook →

4. Mount Fuji via Kawaguchiko

The best views of Japan's most iconic volcano, surrounded by beautiful lakes, onsens, and the Chureito Pagoda.

5. Shibuya Crossing & Shibuya Sky (Tokyo)

Experience the world's busiest intersection, then take the elevator up to Shibuya Sky for a breathtaking open-air view of the metropolis.

6. Tokyo Disneyland & DisneySea

DisneySea is unique to Japan with incredible theming, while Disneyland offers classic magic.

Book Disney Tickets →

7. Dotonbori (Osaka)

The neon-lit heart of Osaka, famous for the Glico Running Man sign and incredible street food.

8. Arashiyama Bamboo Grove (Kyoto)

A serene walk through towering bamboo stalks. Pair it with a visit to the nearby Tenryu-ji Temple.

9. Miyajima Island & Itsukushima Shrine (Hiroshima)

Famous for its "floating" torii gate and friendly wild deer.

10. Shirakawa-go (Gifu)

A picturesque traditional village known for its thatched-roof houses (Gassho-zukuri), especially stunning in winter.
