Top 10 Things to Buy in Japan (Souvenirs)

1. Japanese Skincare & Cosmetics

Brands like Shiseido, SK-II, Hada Labo, and Biore UV sunscreen. Buy them tax-free at drugstores like Matsumoto Kiyoshi or Don Quijote.

2. Matcha and Green Tea

Authentic matcha powder from Uji (Kyoto), or simply delicious matcha-flavored snacks like KitKats and Pocky.

3. Japanese Knives (Hocho)

World-renowned for their sharpness and craftsmanship. Best bought in Kappabashi (Tokyo) or Sakai (Osaka).

4. Anime & Manga Merchandise

Figures, plushies, and exclusive merch from Akihabara (Tokyo), Den Den Town (Osaka), or Jump Shops nationwide.

5. Stationery

High-quality pens, notebooks, and washi tape from stores like Itoya, Tokyu Hands, or Loft.

6. Traditional Crafts

Chopsticks (hashi), ceramics, tenugui (cotton towels), and folding fans (sensu).

7. Electronics & Gadgets

Cameras, watches (Casio, Seiko), and unique gadgets from Yodobashi Camera or Bic Camera. Remember to check voltage and warranty for your home country.

8. Omamori (Temple Amulets)

Beautiful silk amulets sold at shrines and temples, offering protection for travel, health, or success.

9. Japanese Snacks (Dagashi & Omiyage)

Tokyo Banana, Shiroi Koibito cookies, and unique flavors of chips and candies.

10. Second-hand Designer Goods

Japan's strict counterfeit laws and careful owners make it the best place in the world to buy authenticated, pristine second-hand designer bags and watches.
