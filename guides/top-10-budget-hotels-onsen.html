Top 10 Budget Hotels & Onsens

Book Smart

Always check multiple sites to find the best deal.

Search on Agoda Search on Booking.com

1. Dormy Inn (Nationwide)

The king of business hotels. Almost every location features a natural hot spring (onsen) and free ramen late at night (Yonaki Soba).

2. APA Hotels (Nationwide)

Compact rooms, but extremely convenient locations right next to stations. Often includes public baths.

3. Toyoko Inn (Nationwide)

Very affordable, consistent quality, and includes a free Japanese-style breakfast at every location.

4. Super Hotel (Nationwide)

Eco-friendly business hotel featuring public baths, healthy organic breakfasts, and a pillow-selection bar.

5. Nine Hours (Capsule Hotel - Cities)

A sleek, futuristic, and highly standardized capsule hotel experience. Perfect for solo travelers.

6. Daiwa Roynet Hotels (Nationwide)

Slightly more spacious rooms than standard business hotels, excellent cleanliness, and great amenities.

7. First Cabin (Capsule Hotel)

Airplane-themed "First Class" capsule hotels. You get a small room with a bed and high ceilings instead of a traditional capsule tube.

8. K's House Hostels (Various Locations)

Award-winning backpacker hostels, offering a mix of dorms and private rooms with great communal kitchens.

9. Hotel Mystays (Nationwide)

Great value mid-range hotels. Some locations offer apartment-style rooms with kitchenettes.

10. Richmond Hotels (Nationwide)

Known for excellent customer service and comfortable, well-equipped rooms at a reasonable price point.
